# Submission rejected

## DRAFT REJECTED

**SPAR verdict:** `reject_critical` (3-0 unanimous reject).

## Proposed thesis (rejected)

> To determine whether metformin (MET) can improve the immune response to the pneumococcal conjugate vaccine (PCV-13) in older adults.

## Panel reviews

### `evidence_auditor` — `reject` (score 1)

The claim [C071] is marked as 'indirect' and relies on citation [31]. However, the citation trace for [31] reveals that the associated trial (NCT03713801) has no published results (has_results=False). Because there are no published results available in the source, there is no evidence to support the claim that metformin improves the immune response to the PCV-13 vaccine. The claim is essentially an objective/hypothesis of a trial rather than a supported finding.

**Flagged claims:** C071

### `domain_skeptic` — `reject` (score 2)

The submission is fundamentally flawed as it presents a research objective (a 'To determine whether...' statement) as a claim of fact or a finding. A thesis statement describing the goal of a study is not a scientific conclusion and cannot be 'supported' by a citation in the context of a research review panel. Furthermore, the supporting evidence provided is a clinical trial (NCT03713801) that is listed as 'completed' but has no published results. Claiming a potential effect based on a trial that has not yet released its data is the definition of over-claiming and extrapolation.

**Flagged claims:** C071

### `final_judge` — `reject` (score 1)

I align with both the Evidence Auditor and the Domain Skeptic. The submission fails on two fundamental levels: first, the thesis and claim [C071] are phrased as a research objective ('To determine whether...') rather than a factual assertion or a finding. A goal is not a claim that can be validated by evidence. Second, the supporting citation [31] refers to a clinical trial (NCT03713801) that, while completed, has no published results. There is zero empirical data provided to support any conclusion regarding metformin's effect on the PCV-13 vaccine response. The submission is essentially attempting to pass off a study's hypothesis as a proven result.

**Flagged claims:** C071
