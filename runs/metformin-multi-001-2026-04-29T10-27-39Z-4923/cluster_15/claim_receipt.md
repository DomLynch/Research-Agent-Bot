## Panel reviews

### `evidence_auditor` — `reject` (score 3)

The submission contains a failed trace for claim C017. Specifically, the alias match for 'Senescence' failed to resolve, indicating a drift between the claim's terminology and the provided evidence. According to the auditor guidelines, any failed trace is grounds for rejection.

### `domain_skeptic` — `reject` (score 3)

The submission suffers from severe mechanism inflation and extrapolation. Claim C015 makes a bold comparative assertion regarding human postmenopausal women and premenopausal women, yet the supporting evidence (Ref 7) is heavily reliant on murine models, as evidenced by C017 which explicitly mentions 'aged mice'. The leap from mouse macrophage subpopulations to human ovarian collagen organization is an over-claim of the highest order.

Furthermore, C018 frames the ability of metformin to 'prevent age-associated ovarian fibrosis' as a general mechanistic fact. This is an over-scoped conclusion; preclinical results in mice cannot be extrapolated to a definitive preventative claim in humans without clinical trial data. The submission fails to provide any caveats regarding the species gap or the limitations of the sample size in the human cohort mentioned in C015.

**Flagged claims:** C015, C016, C018

### `final_judge` — `reject` (score 2)

I align with both the Evidence Auditor and the Domain Skeptic. The submission exhibits a critical failure in scientific rigor by conflating murine data with human outcomes. While the Evidence Auditor correctly identified a technical trace failure regarding 'Senescence' in C017, the Domain Skeptic's point regarding species extrapolation is the more damning flaw. Claim C015 presents a comparative result in human postmenopausal women, yet the mechanistic support provided in C016, C017, and C018 relies on mouse models. 

Presenting the modulation of macrophage subpopulations in mice as the mechanistic basis for collagen organization in human ovaries without explicit qualification is an unacceptable leap in logic. The submission attempts to establish a general preventative mechanism (C018) based on preclinical evidence, which constitutes severe mechanism inflation. Because the core thesis relies on this flawed extrapolation, the submission cannot be accepted.

**Flagged claims:** C015, C016, C017, C018
