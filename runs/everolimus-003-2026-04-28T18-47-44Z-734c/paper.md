**Dissent — `domain_skeptic`:** The submission presents a fundamental contradiction between C019 and C020 that is not reconciled in the text. C019 explicitly states that the efficacy of RTB101 10 mg in combination with everolimus 'did not meet statistical significance,' yet C020 immediately claims a 'statistically significant reduction' in laboratory-confirmed RTIs for the exact same treatment group (RTB101 10 mg) using a p-value of 0.02. 

From a domain perspective, this suggests a failure to distinguish between primary endpoints and secondary/exploratory endpoints. If the primary endpoint failed (as implied by C019), claiming a significant result in a specific sub-metric (C020) without explicitly labeling it as a secondary or post-hoc analysis is a form of 'cherry-picking' or mechanism inflation. The submission frames a specific laboratory outcome as a general success while simultaneously admitting the overall efficacy failed, which is misleading and over-scoped.
