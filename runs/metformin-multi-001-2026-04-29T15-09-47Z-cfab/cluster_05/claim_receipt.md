# The change in whole-body insulin sensitivity was correlated to the change in mitochondrial respiration

**SPAR verdict:** `accept_clean` (3-0 unanimous accept).

## Thesis

The change in whole-body insulin sensitivity was correlated to the change in mitochondrial respiration [2].

## Background

Metformin attenuated the increase in whole-body insulin sensitivity and VO2 max after AET [2].
Metformin also abrogated the exercise-mediated increase in skeletal muscle mitochondrial respiration [2].

## References
