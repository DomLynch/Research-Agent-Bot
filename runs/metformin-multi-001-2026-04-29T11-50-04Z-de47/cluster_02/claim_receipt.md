## Proposed thesis (rejected)

> Both metabolic and nonmetabolic pathways were significantly influenced, including pyruvate metabolism and DNA repair in muscle and PPAR and SREBP signaling, mitochondrial fatty acid oxidation, and collagen trimerization in adipose.

## Panel reviews

### `evidence_auditor` — `reject` (score 2)

The submission fails the evidence audit because it provides no source abstracts or published results for the cited reference [14]. As the Evidence Auditor, I cannot verify the numeric values in C028 (647 genes, 146 genes) or the specific pathway claims in C029 (pyruvate metabolism, DNA repair, etc.) without the source text. All claims are marked as 'direct', yet there is no evidence provided to trace these assertions back to a published result.

**Flagged claims:** C027, C028, C029

### `domain_skeptic` — `reject` (score 4)

The submission suffers from significant mechanism inflation and a lack of necessary caveats regarding the nature of the evidence. Claim C029 frames the 'influence' of metabolic and nonmetabolic pathways as a direct outcome, but based on the context of C028 (differential gene expression), these are transcriptomic associations, not proven functional changes in pathway activity. There is a critical leap from 'differentially expressed genes' to 'significantly influenced pathways' without accounting for whether these changes translate to protein levels or actual metabolic flux.

Furthermore, the submission fails to specify the population or the duration of the metformin therapy in the claims. Given that DNA repair and collagen trimerization are complex processes, claiming they were 'significantly influenced' based solely on a gene expression screen (FDR < 0.05) is an over-claim. The evidence is correlative/transcriptomic, but the phrasing suggests a direct mechanistic effect.

**Flagged claims:** C029

### `final_judge` — `reject` (score 2)

I align with both the Evidence Auditor and the Domain Skeptic. The submission fails on two fundamental levels: verification and interpretation. First, the complete absence of source text for reference [14] makes it impossible to verify the specific numeric counts in C028 or the pathway identifications in C029, rendering the claims unfalsifiable within the provided context. Second, as the Domain Skeptic correctly noted, there is a significant logical leap in C029. The transition from differential gene expression (transcriptomics) to the claim that pathways were 'significantly influenced' is an overstatement of the evidence, as gene expression does not always correlate with functional protein activity or metabolic flux.

Because the Evidence Auditor flagged the lack of traceability and the Domain Skeptic flagged the mechanistic inflation, there is no path to acceptance. The claims are presented as high-certainty direct effects without the necessary caveats regarding the nature of the data (transcriptomic vs. functional) or the supporting evidence to back the specific numbers cited.

**Flagged claims:** C027, C028, C029
