## Panel reviews

### `evidence_auditor` — `reject` (score 1)

The submission contains a critical failure in the citation trace for claim C012. The alias match for 'Collagen' resolved to 'CAPTOPRIL', which is a completely different pharmacological agent and unrelated to the structural protein collagen. This indicates a fundamental failure in the evidence trace, as the claim regarding collagen content is being supported by data related to an unrelated drug.

**Flagged claims:** C012

### `domain_skeptic` — `reject` (score 2)

The submission suffers from a critical failure in domain alignment and evidence mapping. Specifically, the alias match for C012 resolves 'Collagen' to 'CAPTOPRIL'. Captopril is an ACE inhibitor, not metformin. This indicates a severe 'off-topic drift' where evidence from a completely different pharmacological agent is being used to support claims about metformin's effect on muscle senescence and collagen content.

Furthermore, the claims (C011-C013) describe complex mechanistic outcomes—myofiber atrophy, SASP markers, and FAP senescence—but the provided traces suggest a fundamental mismatch in the underlying data. Extrapolating results from an ACE inhibitor to metformin is scientifically invalid and constitutes a total failure of scoping.

**Flagged claims:** C012

### `final_judge` — `reject` (score 1)

I align with both the Evidence Auditor and the Domain Skeptic. The citation trace for C012 contains a catastrophic error where 'Collagen' was resolved as an alias for 'CAPTOPRIL'. Captopril is an ACE inhibitor and bears no pharmacological or structural relationship to collagen or the primary subject of the thesis, metformin. This is not a minor mapping error but a fundamental failure of evidence integrity, as the claim is being supported by data from an unrelated drug.

Because the thesis (C012) is directly dependent on this flawed mapping, the entire submission is invalidated. There is no reasonable way to weigh the dissent because there was no dissent; both prior reviewers correctly identified a critical failure in the evidence chain that renders the mechanistic claims scientifically baseless.
