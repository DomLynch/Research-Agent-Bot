# We found a statistically significant reduction in the proportion of patients who had one or more laboratory-confirmed RTIs in the RTB101 10 mg once daily treatment group (34 [19%] of 176) compared with the pooled placebo group (50 [28%] of 180; odds ratio [OR] 0·601 [90% CI 0·391-0·922]; p=0·02)

**SPAR verdict:** `accept_caveated` (2-1 accept).

**Dissent — `domain_skeptic`:** The submission presents a critical contradiction between C020 and C021 that is not reconciled in the claims, leading to a misleading representation of efficacy. C020 claims a 'statistically significant reduction' in laboratory-confirmed RTIs, while C021 notes no reduction in 'clinically symptomatic respiratory illness.' In a clinical context, a reduction in lab-confirmed cases that does not translate to a reduction in symptomatic illness suggests that the 'significant' finding in C020 may be clinically irrelevant or driven by asymptomatic detection rather than patient benefit.

Furthermore, the use of a 90% Confidence Interval (CI) instead of the standard 95% CI in C020 is a red flag for 'p-hacking' or marginal significance. By lowering the confidence threshold, the authors are framing a borderline result as a definitive success. The submission fails to provide the necessary caveat that the primary clinical outcome (symptomatic illness) was not met, effectively over-claiming the utility of RTB101 by highlighting a secondary laboratory endpoint while burying the clinical failure.

## Thesis

we found a statistically significant reduction in the proportion of patients who had one or more laboratory-confirmed RTIs in the RTB101 10 mg once daily treatment group (34 [19%] of 176) compared with the pooled placebo group (50 [28%] of 180; odds ratio [OR] 0·601 [90% CI 0·391-0·922]; p=0·02) [7].

## Findings

efficacy of RTB101 10 mg in combination with everolimus 0·1 mg once daily compared with placebo did not meet statistical significance [7].
RTB101 did not reduce the proportion of patients with clinically symptomatic respiratory illness (134 [26%] of 511 patients in the RTB101 treatment group vs 125 [25%] 510 patients in the placebo treatment group; OR 1·07 [90% CI 0·80-1·42]; p=0·65) [7].
