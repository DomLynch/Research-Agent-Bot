# CT scan showed that increases in thigh muscle area (p = .005) and density (p = .020) were greater in placebo versus metformin

**SPAR verdict:** `accept_clean` (3-0 unanimous accept).

## Thesis

CT scan showed that increases in thigh muscle area (p = .005) and density (p = .020) were greater in placebo versus metformin [1].

## Findings

placebo gained more lean body mass (p = .003) and thigh muscle mass (p < .001) than metformin [1].
placebo had decreased type I fiber percentage while metformin did not (p = .007) [1].

## References

[1] Metformin blunts muscle hypertrophy in response to progressive resistance exercise training in older adults: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter trial: The MASTERS trial. (2019). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31557380/
