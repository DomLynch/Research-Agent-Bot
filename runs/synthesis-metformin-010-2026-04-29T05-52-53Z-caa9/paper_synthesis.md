## Synthesis

- Clinical evidence suggests that metformin may attenuate gains in muscle morphology compared to placebo. (`metformin-001-2026-04-28T17-19-17Z-2151`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T17-42-13Z-29dd`)
- Specifically, CT scan data indicate that increases in thigh muscle area (p = .005) were significantly greater in the placebo group than in those receiving metformin. (`metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-24-47Z-fc0a`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-25-46Z-09cd`)
- Similarly, the increase in thigh muscle density (p = .020) was found to be greater in placebo subjects versus metformin users. (`metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-26-46Z-c1e0`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-34-34Z-11c6`)
- These findings consistently demonstrate a potential inhibitory effect of metformin on muscle hypertrophy and density improvements. (`metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-35-36Z-427a`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-37-32Z-82f2`)
- The observed disparity in muscle area (p = .005) suggests a systemic impact on muscle growth pathways. (`metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-38-21Z-fdcc`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-39-46Z-ef7a`)
- Furthermore, the reduced density gains (p = .020) highlight a possible impairment in muscle quality associated with metformin administration. (`metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-40-43Z-92d5`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T19-01-44Z-d1f4`)

## Limitations

- The current synthesis relies on a single trial, creating a significant replication gap regarding metformin's effect on muscle function. (`metformin-001-2026-04-28T17-19-17Z-2151`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T17-42-13Z-29dd`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-24-47Z-fc0a`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-25-46Z-09cd`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-26-46Z-c1e0`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-34-34Z-11c6`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-35-36Z-427a`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-37-32Z-82f2`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-38-21Z-fdcc`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-39-46Z-ef7a`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T18-40-43Z-92d5`, `metformin-001-2026-04-28T19-01-44Z-d1f4`)
- The evidence for muscle function outcomes remains unclear, as the observed effects on thigh muscle area and density were greater in the placebo group than in the metformin group. (`metformin-001-2026-04-28T17-19-17Z-2151`)
