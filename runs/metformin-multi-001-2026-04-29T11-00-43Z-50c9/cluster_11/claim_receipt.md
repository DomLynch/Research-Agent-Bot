# Submission rejected

**SPAR verdict:** `reject_critical` (3-0 unanimous reject).

## Proposed thesis (rejected)

> The objective of this research is to assess the efficacy of oral metformin in mitigating the aging process in middle-aged and elderly males, to pinpoint sensitive indicators of human senescence, and to offer innovative frameworks and scientific insights for pharmaceutical interventions in aging.

## Panel reviews

### `evidence_auditor` — `reject` (score 1)

Claim C074 is marked as 'direct', which requires a published_results citation. However, the citation trace for reference [38] (NCT06459310) explicitly states that the study is still 'recruiting' and 'has_results=False'. A claim cannot be supported by a trial that has not yet produced results, nor can it be a 'direct' evidence claim without a published source.

**Flagged claims:** C074

### `domain_skeptic` — `reject` (score 2)

The submission presents the objective of a clinical trial as a claim of efficacy or a research outcome. However, the supporting evidence (NCT06459310) is for a study that is currently 'recruiting' and has no results. Framing the 'objective' of an ongoing trial as a basis for scientific insight or a claim about mitigating the aging process is a fundamental error in scoping; it mistakes a hypothesis for a result.

**Flagged claims:** C074

### `final_judge` — `reject` (score 1)

Both the Evidence Auditor and the Domain Skeptic correctly identify a fatal flaw in the submission: the claim is supported by a clinical trial (NCT06459310) that is still in the recruiting phase and has produced no results. A research objective or a hypothesis cannot serve as evidence for the efficacy of an intervention. Because the citation trace confirms 'has_results=False', there is no empirical basis to support the claim, regardless of whether it is framed as an objective or a result. I align with the prior reviewers in that this constitutes a fundamental failure of evidence standards.

**Flagged claims:** C074
