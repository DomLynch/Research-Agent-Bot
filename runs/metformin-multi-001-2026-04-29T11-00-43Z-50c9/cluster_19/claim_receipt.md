### `domain_skeptic` — `reject` (score 3)

The submission suffers from severe mechanism inflation and over-claiming. All three claims rely on a single reference ([7]), which, based on the context of 'aged mice' in C016, is a preclinical animal study. The thesis (C017) frames these findings as a 'new strategy to prevent ovarian fibrosis' without any qualification that these results are limited to murine models. Extrapolating the modulation of macrophage subpopulations in mice to a general clinical strategy for human ovarian fibrosis is an unjustified leap.

Furthermore, the claims are tautological and lack specificity. C015 and C017 essentially state the same conclusion twice—that metformin modulates cells to prevent fibrosis—without providing the actual mechanistic pathway or the specific 'unique' characteristics of the macrophage subpopulation mentioned in C016. The leap from observing a change in cell proportions in mice to claiming a validated 'strategy' for preventing a complex human pathological process is over-scoped.

**Flagged claims:** C015, C016, C017
