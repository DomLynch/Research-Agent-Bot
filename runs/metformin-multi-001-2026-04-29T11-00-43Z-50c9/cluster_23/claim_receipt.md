> We conclude that metformin improves autophagy and mitochondrial function largely in parallel to ameliorate a newly defined inflammaging profile that echoes inflammation in diabetes.

## Panel reviews

### `evidence_auditor` — `reject` (score 2)

The submission fails the evidence audit due to a complete lack of source abstracts or published results. While the citation traces indicate a 'PASS' for role matching, there is no actual evidence provided in the input to verify the claims. Specifically, the numeric and qualitative assertions regarding 'Th17 inflammaging profile', 'autophagy', and 'mitochondrial bioenergetics' cannot be traced to any provided text. As an Evidence Auditor, I cannot accept claims that lack the underlying source data to verify the trace of the specific biological mechanisms mentioned.

**Flagged claims:** C001, C002

### `domain_skeptic` — `reject` (score 3)

The submission suffers from severe mechanism inflation and over-claiming. Both C001 and C002 present a complex causal chain—metformin leading to increased autophagy and mitochondrial bioenergetics, which in turn ameliorates a 'newly defined inflammaging profile'—based on a single reference. The leap from a single study to a definitive conclusion about a 'newly defined' profile is premature and lacks the necessary caveats regarding sample size, population specificity, or the distinction between correlation and causation in the mechanistic pathway.

Furthermore, the thesis (C002) frames these processes as occurring 'largely in parallel,' a specific mechanistic claim that requires rigorous temporal and spatial evidence which is not supported by the provided evidence trace. The submission fails to scope the findings to the specific experimental model used in the reference, instead presenting these results as generalizable conclusions about metformin's action on inflammaging.

**Flagged claims:** C001, C002

### `final_judge` — `reject` (score 2)

I align with both the Evidence Auditor and the Domain Skeptic.
