# Submission rejected

## DRAFT REJECTED

**SPAR verdict:** `reject_critical` (3-0 unanimous reject).

## Proposed thesis (rejected)

> To determine whether metformin (MET) can improve the immune response to the pneumococcal conjugate vaccine (PCV-13) in older adults.

## Panel reviews

### `evidence_auditor` — `reject` (score 1)

The claim [C070] is marked as 'indirect' but is phrased as a research objective ('To determine whether...'). However, the citation trace for ref [31] (NCT03713801) explicitly states 'has_results=False'. Because there are no published results for this trial, there is no evidence available to support any assertion regarding the outcome or the validity of the objective in a research context. Furthermore, the claim lacks a published_results citation, which is required for verification of the evidence.

**Flagged claims:** C070

### `domain_skeptic` — `reject` (score 2)

The submission is fundamentally flawed as it presents a research objective (a 'To determine whether...' statement) as a claim of fact or a finding. A thesis statement describing the goal of a study is not a scientific claim that can be supported by evidence; it is a hypothesis or a study aim. 

Furthermore, the supporting evidence (NCT03713801) is a clinical trial that is listed as 'completed' but has no reported results. Attempting to use a trial with no available data to support a claim—even one framed as an objective—is a failure of evidence calibration. There is no evidence provided to suggest that metformin actually improves the immune response; there is only evidence that someone intended to study it.

**Flagged claims:** C070

### `final_judge` — `reject` (score 1)

I align with both the Evidence Auditor and the Domain Skeptic. The submission fails on two fundamental levels: first, it presents a research objective ('To determine whether...') as a claim, which is a category error in research review; a goal is not a finding. Second, the sole supporting reference is a clinical trial (NCT03713801) that has no published results. Consequently, there is zero empirical evidence provided to support the thesis. There is no dissent to weigh in this case, as both prior reviewers correctly identified that the submission provides a study aim without any corresponding data or outcomes.

**Flagged claims:** C070
