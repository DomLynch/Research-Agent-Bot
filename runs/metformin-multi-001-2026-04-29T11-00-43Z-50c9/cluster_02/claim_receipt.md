## Panel reviews

### `evidence_auditor` — `reject` (score 1)

The submission fails the evidence audit because it provides no source abstracts or published results for the cited reference [14]. As the Evidence Auditor, I cannot verify the numeric values in C029 (647 genes, 146 genes) or the specific pathway claims in C030 and C031 without the source text. Since the claims are marked as 'direct' but the actual evidence (the abstract/results of ref 14) is missing from the provided context, the traces cannot be validated beyond the role match.

**Flagged claims:** C028, C029, C030, C031

### `domain_skeptic` — `reject` (score 3)

The submission suffers from severe mechanism inflation and over-claiming. Specifically, C031 frames the identification of 'predictive upstream transcriptional regulators' as a causal explanation ('that may explain') for tissue-specific changes. In transcriptomic studies, these are typically bioinformatic predictions (in silico) rather than experimentally validated mechanistic drivers. Presenting a list of regulators as a 'cascade' without functional validation is an over-reach of the evidence.

Furthermore, C030 lists a wide array of 'significantly influenced' pathways, including DNA repair and collagen trimerization. Without the context of the effect size or the biological relevance of these changes, the claim frames statistical significance as biological significance. The jump from differential gene expression (C029) to the definitive influence of complex metabolic and nonmetabolic pathways (C030) lacks the necessary caveats regarding the gap between mRNA levels and actual protein function or phenotypic outcome.

**Flagged claims:** C030, C031

### `final_judge` — `reject` (score 2)

I align with both the Evidence Auditor and the Domain Skeptic. The primary failure is an evidentiary void; the submission relies entirely on a single reference [14] for all claims, yet the actual content of that reference was not provided for verification. This makes the numeric claims in C029 and the specific pathway lists in C030 impossible to validate, rendering the 'direct' role match meaningless as it only confirms the citation exists, not that it supports the claim.

Furthermore, I find the Domain Skeptic's critique of C031 particularly compelling. The transition from bioinformatic predictions of 'upstream regulators' to a 'cascade' that 'explains' tissue-specific changes is a classic instance of over-claiming in transcriptomic research. Without evidence of functional validation (e.g., knockdown or overexpression studies), these claims remain speculative. The lack of caveats regarding the gap between gene expression and phenotypic outcome in C030 further diminishes the scientific rigor of the submission.

**Flagged claims:** C028, C029, C030, C031
