## Panel reviews

### `evidence_auditor` — `reject` (score 2)

The submission fails the evidence audit because it provides no source abstracts or published results for the cited reference [14]. As the Evidence Auditor, I cannot verify the numeric values in C029 (647 genes, 146 genes) or the specific pathway claims in C030 (pyruvate metabolism, DNA repair, PPAR, SREBP, etc.) without the source text. Since the claims are marked as 'direct' but the supporting evidence is missing from the provided context, the traces cannot be validated beyond the role match.

**Flagged claims:** C028, C029, C030

### `domain_skeptic` — `reject` (score 4)

The submission suffers from significant mechanism inflation and a lack of necessary caveats. Claim C030 frames the 'influence' of metabolic and nonmetabolic pathways as a direct result of metformin therapy, but it relies on differential gene expression (C029). Gene expression is a proxy for potential activity, not a direct measure of pathway flux or functional outcome. Framing 'DNA repair' and 'collagen trimerization' as 'significantly influenced' without providing functional validation or specifying the direction of the effect (upregulation vs. downregulation) is an over-claim.

Furthermore, the submission fails to provide the population context for reference [14]. Without knowing if these results were derived from a small cohort, a specific demographic, or a preclinical model, the claims are over-scoped. The jump from 'differentially expressed genes' to 'significantly influenced pathways' is a common leap in transcriptomic studies that ignores the gap between mRNA levels and actual protein function/metabolic output.

**Flagged claims:** C030

### `final_judge` — `reject` (score 3)

I align with both the Evidence Auditor and the Domain Skeptic. The submission is fundamentally unverifiable because the source text for reference [14] is missing, making the specific numeric claims in C029 and the pathway assertions in C030 impossible to validate. Even if the source were present, the Domain Skeptic correctly identifies a critical flaw in the logic of C030: the leap from differential gene expression to the conclusion that pathways were 'significantly influenced' without functional validation or directional data. This constitutes a significant over-claim typical of transcriptomic data misinterpretation.

Because the Evidence Auditor flagged the lack of source material and the Domain Skeptic flagged the conceptual inflation of the results, there is no basis for acceptance. The lack of population context further renders the claims clinically meaningless.

**Flagged claims:** C028, C029, C030
