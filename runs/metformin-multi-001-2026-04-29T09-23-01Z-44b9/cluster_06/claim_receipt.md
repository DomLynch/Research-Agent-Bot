### `domain_skeptic` — `reject` (score 3)

The submission presents a sweeping, universal negative conclusion ('was not associated') based on a single reference. This is a classic case of over-claiming by failing to scope the finding to a specific population, disease state (e.g., T2DM vs. non-diabetic), or specific neurocognitive domain. Without specifying the study population or the timeframe of the observation, the claim transforms a specific study result into a general biological fact.

Furthermore, the claim lacks necessary caveats regarding dosage, duration of treatment, and the baseline cognitive status of the participants. In neurocognitive research, 'no association' in one cohort does not equate to a general lack of efficacy across the domain, and framing it as a definitive negative outcome without these parameters is scientifically imprecise.

**Flagged claims:** C027
