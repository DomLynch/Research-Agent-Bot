### `domain_skeptic` — `reject` (score 2)

The submission fails on a fundamental level of scoping and logical structure. The 'claim' provided is not a claim at all, but rather a statement of purpose or a research objective ('To determine whether...'). A research goal is not evidence of an effect, and framing a hypothesis as a conclusion is a critical failure in scientific reporting.

Furthermore, the evidence provided is categorized as 'indirect' and 'low' strength, yet it is used to support a thesis that implies a potential therapeutic benefit. There is no actual result or outcome presented to evaluate, making the conclusion entirely unsupported by the provided text. The submission essentially asks a question and presents that question as the finding.

**Flagged claims:** C071

### `final_judge` — `reject` (score 1)

I align with both the Evidence Auditor and the Domain Skeptic. The submission fails on two critical fronts: evidentiary integrity and logical structure. First, the citation trace failed because the provided NCT identifier is non-existent, which constitutes a fatal flaw in the research audit. Second, the 'claim' is phrased as a research objective ('To determine whether...') rather than a finding or a conclusion. A statement of intent cannot serve as a claim of fact or a result.

There is no dissent to weigh in this case, as both prior reviewers identified independent, critical failures. The combination of a fabricated/stale reference and the absence of an actual scientific conclusion makes this submission entirely unsupported.

**Flagged claims:** C071
